DIGITAL ADVERTISING / OUR APPROACH

The Advertisement
Pipeline.

Advertising involves more than launching an ad. Our Advertisement Pipeline connects the planning, campaign experience, and review into one clearly scoped process—so you know what happens at every stage.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the business

Clarify your offering, intended audience, location, objectives, and budget.

02 / PLAN

Build the campaign plan

Choose suitable channels, messaging, creative requirements, and the action you want visitors to take.

03 / CONNECT

Prepare the destination

Link ads to an appropriate website, landing page, contact option, or enquiry experience.

04 / LAUNCH

Run and monitor

Set up agreed campaigns, check delivery and spending, and monitor available performance signals.

05 / LEARN

Report and refine

Explain what the data shows and recommend changes based on the results and agreed goals.

What this means for your business

A coordinated path from visibility to customer action, with clearer accountability at each step. Advertising can create opportunities for enquiries, but sales, bookings, and returns depend on several factors beyond ad delivery.

Transparent costs: The advertising budget paid to the platform is separate from AVORA's planning and management fee. Exact activities and reporting are agreed in the proposal.
